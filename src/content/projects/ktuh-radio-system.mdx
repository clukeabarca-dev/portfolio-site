## Brief

Create a micro-brand for KTUH Radio's fundraising and donation campaign, then carry it across merchandise that supporters would want to keep using.

## Concept

The phrase “The Station That Loves You” became the campaign's voice. A loose headphone illustration, KTUH's red-and-black palette, and repeated language gave the system the energy of independent radio without losing the station's identity.

## System

The system moves from a restrained shirt front to a dense back graphic, then simplifies for caps, mugs, and stickers. Each application retains the headphone mark, station name, and a version of the campaign phrase.

## Outcome

The final family gave KTUH a distinct fundraising identity and a coordinated set of physical rewards for its donation campaign.
