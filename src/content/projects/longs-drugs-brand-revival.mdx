## Brief

Refresh the Longs Drugs brand and website in a way that respects local familiarity while making the system easier to use across digital, retail, and promotional touchpoints. The revival needed to feel like Longs before it asked customers to learn anything new.

## Concept

The direction starts with the working brand guidelines: a clear red brand signal, disciplined logo spacing, a broader island-informed palette, practical typography, and photography that keeps the store environment visible. The goal is not to make Longs feel unfamiliar. It is to sharpen the pieces customers already recognize.

## System

The system connects the brand playbook to real touchpoints: packaging studies, digital screens, printed collateral, storefront graphics, campaign materials, and mobile-first page structure. Every visual decision should help customers move faster through pharmacy, savings, store, wellness, and community information.
