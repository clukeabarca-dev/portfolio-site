## Brief

Build a jewelry packaging system that can move from small-format product cards to a complete gifting experience.

## Concept

Hoku Lei pairs oceanic navy and aqua with delicate botanical linework, warm metallic details, and soft cream paper to make the package feel collected, personal, and gift-ready.

## System

The system extends across windowed earring boxes, necklace inserts, paperboard cartons, tissue, pouches, hang tags, cards, foil marks, and color and material studies.

## Outcome

The case study presents a finished retail package family supported by process studies, dielines, material samples, and intimate product photography.
