## About the archive

This page is a visual archive rather than a case study. The current selection comes from the edited-photo folder and moves between architecture, campus life, gardens, water, and light.

Future original photographs will remain distinct from edited work, so visitors can understand whether an image is being shown as a capture or as a finished color and image-editing study.
