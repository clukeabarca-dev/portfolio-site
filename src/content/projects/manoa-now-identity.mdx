## Brief

Build a brand campaign for Manoa Now, the student-focused marketing and media organization, with enough range for merchandise, digital ads, print promotion, social assets, and app awareness.

## Concept

The central idea was rooted in Manoa. The campaign used the existing mark as a base, then expanded it through color and geometry inspired by Hawaiian quilt work.

## System

The work moved between apparel, posters, bag graphics, campaign photography, and mobile app promotion. For the app campaign, the message became practical: one place for campus resources, updates, and student essentials.

## Outcome

The final system gave Manoa Now a clearer public presence while keeping the pieces flexible enough for a student team to keep using across changing campaigns.
