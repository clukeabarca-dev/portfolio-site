## Brief

Vital View 3D needed a public website quickly so a new breast-imaging center could get off the ground. The first version did not need to behave like a large hospital network. It needed to establish that the center was real, communicate its main point of difference, begin accepting patients, and make the next step obvious.

The live homepage leads with the center's no-compression breast-imaging message and a concise explanation of the scan experience. A short set of care highlights—true 3D imaging, a seven-second scan, and a more comfortable experience—gives visitors the essential information before they reach the scheduling path.

## A deliberately focused launch

The content hierarchy is short by design. The homepage answers the first question, then offers three kinds of next step: learn more, contact the center, or estimate risk through a linked assessment resource. That sequence keeps the page useful to visitors with different levels of readiness.

The lower half of the page supports scheduling and sets expectations for follow-up. A separate contact page creates a simpler route for questions, referral support, or people who are not ready to request an appointment.

## Trust through clarity

Healthcare pages need to feel credible without overwhelming people with technical language. The page uses clear headings, limited claims, direct calls to action, and visible contact information. The visual tone stays calm and approachable while keeping scheduling and provider resources easy to find.

## Outcome

The outcome is a lean launch site that gave Vital View 3D a working public presence at the moment the center needed it. It can expand as the organization grows, but the first version already performs the most important job: helping people understand the offering and move toward the live center.
