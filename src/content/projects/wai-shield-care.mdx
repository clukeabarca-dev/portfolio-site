## Brief

Wai Shield is a local Hawaii product now sold in Longs Drugs stores. The product was created to fill a gap in locally produced care needs, especially for incontinence products that customers may want to shop for discreetly.

## Concept

Because these are incontinence-care products, I wanted the package to feel as discreet as possible while moving away from generic care packaging, including the bold black CVS Health packaging used for similar products.

The name Wai Shield is direct but local: wai means water, and the phrase water shield connects clearly to protection and product function.

## System

The packaging uses clear typography to communicate what the product is and organize the accompanying information so customers do not have to feel awkward in the aisle or while carrying the product through the store.

The mockups focus on the hero package, shelf context, material detail, and design-table proof, keeping the product language calm and retail-ready.

## Outcome

The final page presents Wai Shield as a local care packaging system that is practical, discreet, and easy to understand at shelf level.
