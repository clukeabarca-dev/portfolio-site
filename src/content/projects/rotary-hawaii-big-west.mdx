## Brief

Rotary International was holding a conference in Australia, and I worked with the Western districts, the Big West, to create a small campaign for the event.

## Concept

The campaign included signage, apparel, and a small storytelling gift. The mark was inspired by the Rotary gear, Rotary's service principles, and Indigenous Australian visual culture tied to the conference setting.

I focused on four principles: individual service, unity, guidance, and prosperity. The style of the signage, storytelling, and mark also used dotted visual language as a reference point for the campaign's connection to Australia.

## System

The system connected shirts, event signage, vehicle graphics, printed collateral, and a gift presentation into one campaign language. Rotary purple, green accent color, gear-like forms, and circular composition helped the mark stay connected to the organization while still feeling specific to the Big West event.

## Outcome

The campaign was called Dreams of Progress, and it gave the Big West group a strong, recognizable presence at an international event.
