## Brief

Develop a sub-identity for Rotary's Big West zones in honor of the international convention in Melbourne. The system needed to carry meaning, appear on apparel, and explain itself clearly in a gift package for special guests.

## Concept

The concept, Imagine Progress, adapted Rotary's campaign language into a regional mark. The visual story referenced Australian Aboriginal art and creation-story symbolism through ideas of return, manifestation, unity, guidance, and prosperity.

## System

The mark combined boomerang-inspired forms, circular gathering-place geometry, Rotary purple, and green accent color. Applications included shirts, vehicle graphics, a printed explanation letter, and a folder-style gift presentation.

## Outcome

The final identity gave the Big West group a recognizable presence for the convention and a practical service apparel system that could continue into future events.
