## Brief

Explore a brand identity for Autolux, a self-initiated luxury mobile car detailing concept. The audience is owners of premium vehicles who want the convenience of on-site service, but still need the brand to feel secure, careful, and worth trusting with an expensive car.

## Concept

This started as a fun identity exercise around an everyday service that could feel more elevated. The concept combines automotive luxury with water, shine, and a concierge-like tone. The droplet form suggests cleaning and protection, while the gold-on-black palette gives the mobile service a more premium presence.

## System

The identity includes a primary mark, supporting symbol variations, a simple lockup, atmospheric photography, and service-vehicle graphics. Each piece is meant to signal the same thing: this is a practical detailing company, but it understands the emotional value of a high-end vehicle.

## Outcome

Autolux becomes a compact brand study with a clear point of view: premium detailing should feel precise, discreet, and reliable before the appointment even starts.
