## Brief

Create a brand identity for Autolux Detailing, a luxury auto-detailing company with roots in Honolulu and a growing presence in the Seattle area.

## Concept

The system combines automotive luxury with water, shine, and Hawaiian symbolic references. The droplet form suggests cleansing and care, while gold shifts the identity toward premium service.

## System

The identity includes a primary mark, supporting symbol variations, a simple lockup, atmospheric photography, and service vehicle graphics. Concentric forms represent the relationship between customer, vehicle, and company.

## Outcome

Placed inside the Identity Studies Archive, Autolux becomes a strong example of how a smaller identity can still carry a complete story across mark, mood, message, and real-world application.
