## Brief

Create a single Island Living packaging language for everyday eco paper goods that feels local, useful, and easy to navigate.

## Concept

The system uses a rich green-to-light-yellow gradient, the orange Island Living wordmark, and the supplied hibiscus and monstera artwork as a consistent shelf cue across paper goods, storage, and compostable utensils.

## System

Packaging, pattern placement, color studies, product photography, and retail mockups should feel like one family across 16 oz paper cups, plates, bowls, trays, toilet paper, paper towels, napkins, food wraps, storage bags, cutlery, chopsticks, and compostable containers.

## Outcome

This page highlights how the original cup direction can expand into a complete eco paper goods line while preserving the supplied logo, pattern art, and transparent-background intent.
