## Brief

Create an artist and architecture case study about Shigeru Ban that honors his design philosophy without simply copying his buildings.

## Concept

The product became modular and multi-purpose: a poster, information-card system, and booklet built from a single printed sheet. The material strategy reflected Ban's interest in accessible, recyclable, everyday materials.

## System

The system used newsprint, Helvetica Neue, black ink, and magenta as the only color accent. Folds and panel sequencing created the architecture of the piece, letting the object shift between spread, poster, and compact booklet.

## Outcome

The result is an editorial object about architecture that behaves architecturally itself: economical, modular, accessible, and driven by material decisions.
