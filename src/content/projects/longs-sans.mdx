## Brief

Develop a sans serif typography direction that can support the Longs Drugs brand across signage, web, promotions, and everyday retail communication.

## Concept

Longs Sans is built around clarity, sturdy proportions, and a familiar retail voice.

## System

The case study shows specimens, display sizes, body copy tests, signage use, and website mockups built from Longs homepage, store finder, and weekly ad imagery.

## Outcome

This page should make the type study feel like a real brand asset, not just a lettering exercise.
