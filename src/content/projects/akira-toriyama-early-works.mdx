## Brief

Design an exhibition campaign exploring Akira Toriyama's work before his global Dragon Ball fame, with an emphasis on visit planning, printed invitations, merchandise, and an interactive app experience.

## Concept

For Fun's Sake treats the exhibition as energetic, collectible, and easy to navigate. The graphic language uses yellow, black, badges, bags, and invitation systems to create motion without depending on a single character image.

## System

The system includes invitations, guest and staff badges, exhibition merchandise, tote bags, printed cards, and a mobile app concept. The app supports ticket reservations, merchandise purchases, artwork exploration, and a unique AR layer during the museum visit.

## Outcome

The result is a self-initiated exhibition identity that turns archival research into a complete visitor journey: before the visit, inside the exhibition, and after the merchandise leaves with the audience.
